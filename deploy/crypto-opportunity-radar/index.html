Why the reference winners worked

These are the patterns the model is designed to detect—not claims that every future winner must look identical.

HYPE · value accrual

Strong price leadership was backed by a business producing real fees and programmatic token buybacks, plus rising ecosystem usage.

  • Relative strength vs broad crypto
  • Protocol revenue and holder value accrual
  • Growing OI / platform activity
  • Structural buyback demand

ZEC · catalyst + usage

The rally combined a concrete institutional-access catalyst with renewed privacy demand, rising network activity, heavy volume and derivatives participation.

  • ETF / ETP access and inflows
  • Shielded-supply / privacy usage
  • Breakout relative strength
  • OI and volume confirmation

VVV · improving tokenomics

Venice linked product revenue to buy-and-burn mechanics while reducing emissions, creating a cleaner demand/supply loop around actual AI usage.

  • Revenue-linked burns
  • Falling emissions
  • Product-linked token utility
  • Momentum confirmed by structural scarcity
